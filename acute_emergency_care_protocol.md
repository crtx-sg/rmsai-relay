# Acute & Emergency Care Arrhythmia Protocol
*Reference Standards: AHA/ACC/HRS Emergency Guidelines & ACLS Algorithms*

## 1. Initial Triage & Stabilization (All Arrhythmias)
*   **Assess Hemodynamic Stability:** Evaluate for signs of shock, hypotension, altered mental status, acute heart failure, or ischemic chest discomfort.
*   **Unstable Pathway:** Immediate **synchronized cardioversion** for tachyarrhythmias (AFib, SVT, VT) or **transcutaneous pacing** for unstable bradyarrhythmias.
*   **Stable Pathway:** Establish intravenous access, initiate continuous ECG monitoring, obtain a 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes, and proceed to rhythm-specific management.

## 2. Rhythm-Specific Acute Protocols

### Atrial Fibrillation (AFib) with Rapid Ventricular Response (RVR)
*   **Rate Control:** Administer IV **beta-blockers** (e.g., metoprolol 5 mg IV bolus up to 3 doses) OR IV **diltiazem** (0.25 mg/kg bolus followed by an infusion).
*   **Rhythm Control:** Avoid acute chemical or electrical cardioversion if the AFib duration is >48 hours (or unknown) unless the patient is anticoagulated or cleared via transesophageal echocardiogram (TEE).

### Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT)
*   **First Line:** **Vagal maneuvers** (modified Valsalva or carotid massage).
*   **Second Line:** **IV Adenosine** rapid bolus (6 mg; if no response, escalate to 12 mg).
*   **Third Line:** IV diltiazem, verapamil, or beta-blockers if adenosine terminates or slows the rhythm but fails to sustain sinus conversion.

### Premature Ventricular Complexes (PVCs)
*   **Electrolyte Management:** Immediately correct underlying deficits. Target **K+ > 4.0 mEq/L** and **Mg2+ > 2.0 mEq/L**.
*   **Symptom Control:** Administer IV beta-blockers for highly symptomatic or triggering PVC runs.
