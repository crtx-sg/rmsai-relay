Raising FP_SUPPRESS_MIN_CONFIDENCE publishes more, not less: a normal-sinus read below it is no longer suppressed, it just continues flagged uncertain.

Why the override could not stay as it was

The second arm of the override is an any() across six independent Mann-Kendall trends — HR, SpO₂, systolic, diastolic, respiratory rate, temperature. Six chances to trip one boolean. Measured across 15 events from data/inference/*2026-08*.h5:

HR10 / 15
Respiratory rate7 / 15
SpO₂6 / 15
Diastolic3 / 15
Systolic2 / 15
Temperature0 / 15
Any of the six14 / 15

The one window with no deteriorating trend — AV_BLOCK_2_TYPE2 at 0.54 confidence — came through the other arm on MEWS 3. Between them, 15 of 15 published, the lowest at 0.54, and moving the threshold from 0.60 to 0.80 changed nothing. A threshold that never fires is not a conservative default; it is a setting that reads as protection while providing none. The fix was not to start dropping those patients, but to stop asserting their rhythm.

What changed

  • The row shows its confidence. Each worklist event carries the model's certainty, flagged ⚠ when below LOW_CONFIDENCE_CAVEAT. A coin-flip rhythm read no longer renders identically to a certain one.
  • Vitals no longer raise the confidence bar. An uncertain rhythm is never asserted as a finding, however bad the vitals are.
  • …but a deteriorating patient is never silently withheld. Below the gate the alert is re-based rather than dropped: the row leads with Vitals alert and the named vital, the rhythm is demoted to unconfirmed: <type>, and the spoken call alert says “Possible …, treat the rhythm as unconfirmed”. Only an uncertain rhythm on a patient whose vitals are unremarkable is withheld — and that event is still persisted and answerable in chat.

One predicate decides this for every surface: common.criticality.alert_basis returns ("rhythm", "") or ("vitals", why), and the worklist row, the spoken alert, and the console all lead with what it says. The claim on screen is the claim the relay can actually support.

Applied to the 15 measured events, nothing is lost from the worklist — the two sub-threshold rhythms (SINUS_BRADYCARDIA at 0.59, AV_BLOCK_2_TYPE2 at 0.54) both had vitals that warranted attention, so both still alert, now as vitals-driven rather than as asserted rhythms.
