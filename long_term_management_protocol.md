# Long-Term Arrhythmia Management Protocol
*Reference Standards: ACC/AHA/HRS Longitudinal Care Guidelines*

## 1. Atrial Fibrillation (AFib) Long-Term Care
*   **Thromboembolic Prevention:** Calculate **CHA2DS2-VASc score**. Initiate long-term **DOAC therapy** (apixaban, rivaroxaban) for scores ≥2 in men or ≥3 in women. Refer for LAAO (Watchman) if long-term anticoagulation is contraindicated.
*   **Maintenance Rate Control:** Oral beta-blockers or calcium channel blockers titrated to a resting heart rate <110 bpm.
*   **Maintenance Rhythm Control:** Antiarrhythmic drugs or **Catheter Ablation** (Class 1 indication for symptomatic paroxysmal AFib or AFib with heart failure).
*   **Lifestyle Optimization:** Regular exercise, weight loss, and mandatory treatment of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).

## 2. Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT) Long-Term Care
*   **Conservative Care:** Oral beta-blockers or calcium channel blockers for mild, infrequent episodes.
*   **Definitive Care:** **Catheter Ablation** is recommended as a first-line or early choice for patients with recurrent, symptomatic SVT desiring a permanent cure.

## 3. Premature Ventricular Complexes (PVCs) Long-Term Care
*   **Risk and Burden Assessment:** 24-48 hour Holter monitoring to verify PVC burden percentage. Echocardiogram to evaluate left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF).
*   **Intervention Threshold:** Treat if highly symptomatic or if the burden is **>10-15%** to prevent PVC-induced cardiomyopathy.
*   **Therapeutic Options:**
    *   **First Line:** Oral beta-blockers or nondihydropyridine calcium channel blockers.
    *   **Definitive Therapy:** **Catheter Ablation** for frequent, monomorphic PVCs, particularly if medical therapy is ineffective or if structural heart decline is suspected.
